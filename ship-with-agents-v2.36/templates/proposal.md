# Proposal: `<short-title>`

```
Status: proposed
Owner: <name or role>
Last reviewed: YYYY-MM-DD
```

A proposal is a short, committable document for a change that is not trivial and is not yet a decision. It is cheaper to write than a full ADR and more durable than a chat message.

Keep proposals under two pages when possible. Long proposals are a sign the idea is not ready.

## Summary

One paragraph. What is being proposed, in plain language.

## Problem

What is wrong, missing, confusing, or expensive about the current state. Be specific. Include at least one concrete example.

## Goals

What success looks like. Prefer outcomes over implementations.

- `<outcome 1>`;
- `<outcome 2>`;
- `<outcome 3>`.

## Non-Goals

What this proposal is deliberately not solving. Listing non-goals prevents scope creep later.

- `<non-goal 1>`;
- `<non-goal 2>`.

## Proposed Approach

Enough detail that someone could estimate the work. Not a full implementation spec.

- what changes at the surface level;
- what changes internally;
- what stays the same;
- any migration or backfill needed.

## Alternatives Considered

At least two. A proposal with no alternatives looks like a decision pretending to be a proposal.

- **Alternative A:** `<short description>` — why not.
- **Alternative B:** `<short description>` — why not.

## Risks

Honest list. If there are no risks, the proposal is probably too small to need one.

- `<risk>` — mitigation: `<what>`;
- `<risk>` — mitigation: `<what>`.

## Open Questions

Things that must be answered before the proposal becomes a decision.

- `<question>`;
- `<question>`.

## Decision Path

How this moves forward.

- who needs to approve;
- what information is still missing;
- what happens if it is rejected (archive? revisit later? replace with what?).

## If Accepted

- becomes: `<ADR-number-or-task-packet-or-both>`;
- affects: `<docs, code surfaces, AGENTS.md, etc.>`;
- follow-up work: `<bulleted list>`.
