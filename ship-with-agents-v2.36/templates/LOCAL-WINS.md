# Local Wins

Use this file in `_agent-system-local/` for buyer-specific patterns that
worked unusually well and should be reused in your own setup.

Examples:

- a strong review loop your team prefers
- a local launch pattern that reduced friction
- a prompt structure that works especially well in your runtime

## Rule

- If the pattern is local, keep it here.
- If the pattern looks broadly reusable, decide later whether it belongs
  in the vendor package, your own fork, or just your team setup.
