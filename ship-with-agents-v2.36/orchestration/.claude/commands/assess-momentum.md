description: Assess whether work is actually moving or only durably routed
---

Assess current momentum for the relevant lane or workstream.

Read:

1. the relevant row in `health/workstreams.json`
2. the current slice, checkpoint, or closeout
3. the relevant inbox and update index

Answer:

- who owns the next move
- whether pickup is `active`, `pending`, `nudge-now`, `stale`, or `closed`
- whether `No user action needed:` is honest right now
- what the smallest honest pickup trigger is, if one is needed

Refresh the workstream row if pickup truth or next action changed.
