# System Story Digest

Use this when the system needs one compact evolving story of itself.

## Core Truth

The control plane should not force head, manager, or doctor to synthesize the
whole organism from scratch every time.

## Keep This Compact

A good system story answers:

- what changed recently
- what is moving now
- what is blocked
- what is at risk
- what should happen next

## Suggested Sections

- `Recent meaningful changes`
- `Hot workstreams`
- `New blockers or cleared blockers`
- `Conflicts or merge opportunities`
- `Best next system move`

## Final Rule

If the system cannot tell a short truthful story about itself, it will feel
smart but disconnected.
