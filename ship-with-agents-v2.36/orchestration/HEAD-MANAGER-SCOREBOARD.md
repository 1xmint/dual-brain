# Head-Manager Scoreboard

Use this as the compact quality scoreboard for the top of the chain.

## Goal

Make top-chain quality legible without rereading whole transcripts.

## Scoreboard Fields

- `decisionSharpness`
- `sequencingQuality`
- `topologyFit`
- `managerLoad`
- `superAuditQuality`
- `bridgeDiscipline`
- `buyerLaborLeakage`
- `closeoutTransitionQuality`
- `budgetRoutingClarity`
- `frustrationResponsiveness`

Use `green`, `yellow`, or `red`.

## What Green Means

- head decisions clearly explain why now, not now, and next owner
- manager audits materially improve super outputs
- handoffs include executable bridges when needed
- execution completion moves cleanly into closeout truth
- buyer frustration is noticed and either resolved or explicitly tracked

## What Red Means

- top-chain lanes keep producing polite but incomplete movement
- buyers repeatedly ask who should do what next
- managers pass through weak super packets
- budget or provider reality is ignored
- frustration patterns repeat without becoming doctor truth

## Refresh Rule

Refresh this scoreboard when:

- a doctor audit on top-chain quality finishes
- a major workflow release lands
- repeated top-chain failure patterns appear or disappear

## Final Rule

If the system feels smart in the middle but sloppy at the top, check this
scoreboard before changing lower-layer behavior.
