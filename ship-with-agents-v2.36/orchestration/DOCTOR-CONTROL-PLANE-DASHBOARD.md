# Doctor Control-Plane Dashboard

Use this as the compact doctor-facing summary of system awareness.

## Purpose

Compress the whole-system survey into one fast read.

## Sections

- overall doctor visibility
- orphan or half-born lane risk
- stale control-plane surfaces
- unresolved frustrations
- foreign-result absorption risk
- unlogged meaningful turns
- weak lane-awareness scores
- top repeated failure patterns
- conflict hot spots
- opportunity hot spots
- best next repair

## Final Rule

If doctor needs five files just to answer "what is most broken right now?",
the dashboard is not doing its job.
