# External Research Evidence Ledger

Use this to store durable external findings without bloating every lane.

## Record

- question
- freshness level (`F0-F3`)
- sources used
- what changed because of research
- what remains uncertain
- refresh date

## Good Use Cases

- architecture research
- docs verification
- security-sensitive guidance
- pricing or platform capability checks
- product or market scout passes

## Final Rule

Store the durable research truth, not a giant transcript of browsing.
