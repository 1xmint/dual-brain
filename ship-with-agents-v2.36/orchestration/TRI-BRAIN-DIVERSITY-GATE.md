# Tri-Brain Diversity Gate

Use this before adding a third brain or third provider surface.

## Core Truth

Three-brain mode is for independent value, not ornamental thoroughness.

## Good Reasons

- audited launch where one extra independent challenge could catch a costly miss
- provider-diverse review would likely detect a different failure class
- one review lane is context-pure but still too close to the execution plan
- a specialist audit or doctor lane is clearly justified

## Bad Reasons

- "we have another subscription"
- "more brains sounds stronger"
- turning normal `T3` work into a parade of shallow checks

## Required Questions

1. what unique check does the third brain provide?
2. why can the first two brains not cover it cleanly?
3. is the overhead worth it here?
4. should this be a durable lane or a bounded audit?

## Final Rule

If the third brain cannot be named by its unique function, do not add it.
